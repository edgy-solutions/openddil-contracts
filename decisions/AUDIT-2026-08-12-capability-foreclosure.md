# Audit — capability foreclosure sweep (ADR-0038 C1–C7)

**Date:** 2026-08-12 · **Box:** reading task, one table, no fixes
**Commissioned by:** ADR-0038 §What this ADR did not establish — *"no
sweep for foreclosure was run… the absence of a violation is not evidence
of none."*

## Why

ADR-0038's seven do-not-harden constraints were derived by reasoning
about three anticipated capabilities against known designs. The codebase
was never read for assumptions that already violate them. AE-2 — the
recommendation-shaped field — was found **incidentally** while writing
that ADR, which is the base-rate argument that siblings exist. The
fallback-honesty sweep is the precedent: a suspicion about one surface
became a population when someone looked.

Scope: `openddil-contracts/proto` (all 15), the OSS services
(`logistics-fusion`, `cm-service`, `projector`, `asset-registry-service`,
`tactical-agents`, `hq`), and the frontend where a constraint reaches
presentation.

**Result: not clean.** One constraint is already past due rather than
pending, and the ADR that commissioned this sweep carries two claims the
sweep falsifies.

## F1 — the finding that changes ADR-0038

**Block 6 advisory generation is not absent. It exists, it is rule-based,
and it has been shipping.**

`cm-service`'s discrepancy analyzer compares an authorized baseline
against as-maintained state and emits a `ConfigurationDiscrepancy` per
finding — each carrying a machine-composed `recommended_action`:

- *"Install authorized part in slot {slot_id}"*
- *"Replace with authorized part: one of …"*
- *"Perform inspection to record installed CI in …"*
- *"Upgrade CI to revision …"*
- *"Schedule application of {mod_id}"*
- *"OVERDUE: apply {mod_id} immediately"* / *"Apply {mod_id} before {due}"*

These persist (`as_maintained/store.py`), and they surface in **two**
places: the maintainer drill-in (`CmStateCard.tsx:235`) and an HQ panel
rolled up enterprise-wide, worst-CM-status first — `HqWorkOrders.tsx`,
whose rendered title is **"Enterprise CM Recommendations."**

That is ISO 13374 block 6: a recommended action attached to an assessed
condition, generated by machine, presented to an operator.

**What it lacks is exactly what C4 was written to protect.**
`Discrepancy.recommended_action` (`discrepancy.proto:38`) is a bare
`string`. No producer, no version, no `config_hash`, no confidence, no
statement of what the advisory does not claim. The rule set that produced
it is not identifiable from the record. *Why was this advised* is
answerable today only by reading the analyzer's source at the revision
that was deployed.

**So C4 is not a constraint awaiting its first advisory. It is a
constraint that was needed before the advisories that already exist.**
Its stated trigger — *"before the first advisory-producing unit is
registered"* — has passed. The cost is no longer "near zero if decided
now": there is a populated field, two consumers, and a persistence layer.
Still small, and smaller now than after the analytics registry lands and
multiplies producers.

**One thing the sweep found done right, and it matters.**
`HqWorkOrders.tsx` was renamed from a work-order-system title *per
ADR-0017's honesty discipline*, with a comment stating plainly that the
panel does not talk to a work-order system and the data is CM-derived.
The provenance of the **panel** was corrected. The provenance of the
**advisories inside it** was never in scope. That is the gap the naming
fix could not reach — and it is why F1 survived a discipline that has
otherwise been applied thoroughly to this exact component.

## F2 / F3 — the horizon exists, and it renders without a basis

`_eval_mtbf` reads `ComponentWearTrend.remaining_useful_life`'s latest
value and slope, computes `hours_to_zero = latest / -slope`, and emits it
as `projected_time_to_worse`. `compose_status` folds the soonest
non-critical factor's horizon into
`projected_mission_capable_remaining`; the projector writes it to
`..._seconds`; the maintainer card renders it as **"Projected
mission-capable: 5h 58m"**.

Two consequences.

**For C1 (good news):** the forward-looking time basis the constraint
protects **already exists in the logistics envelope** and is exercised
end to end. C1's cost claim is unchanged — ADR-0034's *detection-event*
envelope is still unbuilt — but the substrate argument in ADR-0038 §3 is
stronger than written: this project has already shipped a horizon
through proto, stream, store, and screen.

What is absent everywhere is the **uncertainty band**. `confidence` is a
scalar on `ConstrainingFactor` and is not populated by the horizon
producer (F4). There is no interval type in `common/v1/quantity.proto`.

**For ADR-0035 (bad news):** the rendering is a bare formatted duration
with no basis marker. The value is a linear extrapolation of a slope
whose coefficients are authored placeholders explicitly documented as
unvalidated (ADR-0020). Under ADR-0035's classes this is a
*modelled-not-measured* value rendered as a plain fact — class 1's
`SYNTHESIZED` treatment, or an equivalent, is missing.

Worth noting where it sits: `LogisticsStatusCard` is otherwise
**exemplary** — a syncing notice that never falls through to empty copy, a
genuinely-empty state, and a backfill state that renders its own
rationale. One field in a well-disciplined component. **Same shape as
IH-1**, which was a plausible zero in the component whose sentinel
handling was otherwise correct. Two instances now of *the honesty
discipline applied at component level, with one field inside it
untouched* — which suggests the unit of review should be the field, not
the component.

## What this audit did not establish

- **No overlay, egress, or reasoning-plane material was read.** Customer
  bundles, egress mappings, and ADR-0031's seams are out of scope, as
  they were for AUDIT-2026-08-08. F1's shape is likely to recur in egress
  translation, unchecked.
- **`logistics-sim` and the test suites were not swept.** Synthetic
  producers may carry advisory-shaped output that would move F1's
  population count.
- **No runtime observation.** Everything here is read from source. Whether
  the horizon is populated *in practice* for real assets — as opposed to
  in the unit test that pins it — was not checked on a cluster.
- **C1 was assessed against the two envelopes that exist**, not against
  every message that could become a detection event. `inventory_events`
  and `windowed_telemetry` were read for shape but not analysed for
  forward-looking capacity.
- **No fix was applied, per the box.** F1 and F3 are both small and both
  deliberately left.

## Conclusions

- **The sweep paid.** One violation (F1) that inverts a claim in the ADR
  commissioning it, one presentation-honesty violation (F3), and four
  watch-items. Predicted by AE-2's incidental discovery; consistent with
  the fallback sweep's yield.
- **C4 moves from *pending* to *overdue*.** Its trigger has passed.
- **ADR-0038 requires correction, not annotation** — §1's block-6 row and
  AE-2 both state the opposite of what ships. Corrected in the same
  change as this audit.
- **F3 is an ADR-0035 register row**, not a C1–C7 finding; it is recorded
  here because the sweep found it and losing it to a scope boundary would
  be the failure ADR-0037 §6 exists to prevent.
